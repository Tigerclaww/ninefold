MIND READER

Roll the dice, then add the top numbers together.

If you have a double digit, add them together to create a single digit.

Subtract 5.

Think of a country that corresponds with the letter of the alphabet at that position.

I shall now read your mind.

SENSING…
WELCOME TO DENMARK

Subtract 2.

Think of a country known for its architecture — the letter of the alphabet at that position will guide you there.

I shall now read your mind.

SENSING…
WELCOME TO FRANCE
